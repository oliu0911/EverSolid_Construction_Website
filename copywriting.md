# EverSolid Construction — Copywriting (Revised)

改寫目的:原文案句型偏 AI 生成腔調(三詞逗號串接 + em dash 收尾),且 Commercial 四個子類別描述只在形容空間本身,沒點出「我們蓋的」這件事,跟 What We Build 區塊主旨脫節。以下改成更口語、更直接、以「We」開頭的句子,並在每句補回「建造」動作。

## Section 02 — What We Build

**導言**
- 原文:Residential and commercial construction across Belize — planned well, communicated clearly, and delivered to last.
- 改為:We build homes and businesses across Belize — done right, built to last.

**Residential**
- 原文:Custom homes and renovations built to last generations
- 改為:We build and renovate homes that last generations.

**Commercial 子類別**

| 類別 | 原文 | 改為 |
|---|---|---|
| Supermarket | Aisles, cold storage, and checkouts built for high turnover. | We build supermarkets ready for busy days. |
| Restaurant | Kitchens, dining rooms, and front-of-house built for service. | We build restaurant kitchens and dining rooms, ready to open and serve. |
| Hardware | Retail and storage spaces built for heavy stock and steady traffic. | We build hardware stores that hold heavy stock and handle daily foot traffic. |
| Hotel | Rooms, common areas, and resilience built for guests and climate. | We build hotel rooms and common areas made for Belize's climate. |

## Why Choose Us

**導言**
- 原文:Four reasons Belize homeowners and builders stay with EverSolid.
- 改為:Why Belize homeowners and businesses keep coming back to EverSolid.

**四大重點**

| 重點 | 原文 | 改為 |
|---|---|---|
| Fast & Professional Construction | We deliver projects on schedule without cutting corners — speed and quality are not trade-offs. | We finish on time. We don't cut corners. |
| Planning & Material Guidance | We help you make the right choices for your project — from materials to methods, before ground breaks. | We help you pick the right materials and methods before we break ground. |
| Great Communication | Clear updates, honest timelines, and direct access. You are never left wondering what is happening. | You'll always know what's going on — clear updates, honest timelines, direct access to us. |
| Over 20 Years of Experience | Two decades of building in Belize. We know the climate, the regulations, and the people. | 20 years building in Belize. We know the climate, the rules, and the people. |
